# Surface brief: Shetu web app (apps/web)

Scope: the whole signed-in app (overview, leaves list, leave workspace, people, audit log). Mode: Operate. Desktop-first; employee screens usable on mobile.
Audience and job: managers and HR plan, check and approve cover and close leaves; the employee does the interview and the return decision; covering colleagues report changes. Judges see it in a 3-minute video.
Constraints: brand pinned in PRODUCT.md (crimson arch, deck palette, Plus Jakarta Sans, light only, no emoji). Synthetic data labelled on every screen.

## Direction contract
seed: 0ddb0d5442e0e8558f63b1622ef8a385 (surface roll; dealt lead "the bridge"; challenger fused: boarding-pass and gate board, for change rows that stay lit until acknowledged)

THESIS: A leave is a bridge with four piers. The workspace opens on that bridge, so the manager sees where the leave stands and what blocks it before reading a single table. Refuses the KPI-tile dashboard with a tabbed detail page.

OWN-WORLD: Deep crimson sidebar rail with blush text; paper ground; white panels with hairline borders; ink type in Plus Jakarta Sans at product density (13 to 14px body, tabular figures). Crimson for action and blocking state, leaf green for passing rules, gold for pending, rose hangers as quiet structure.

STORY: Overview tells the manager what needs them now; opening a leave shows the bridge, the stage's work, and the rules verdict side by side; every AI statement shows its evidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Leave workspace: sidebar, breadcrumb bar with persona menu, leave header, full-width arch with four piers carrying status and counts, then stage work area (left) and rules inspector (right) above the fold at 1440.

SIGNATURE: The arch timeline. The deck between piers fills crimson as stages complete; a blocked pier turns crimson with its blocking rule; piers are the stage tabs. Changes during leave stay highlighted until acknowledged.

RISK: The arch must stay a working control (keyboard tabs, real states), not decoration; on mobile it collapses to a four-step strip.
